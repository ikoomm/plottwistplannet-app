Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Plot Twist Planet Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @plottwistplannet for that account only. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

  • Basic TikTok account information needed to confirm which account is connected (for example the account's display name and open ID).
  • Permission to upload the account owner's own videos to their TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload). The owner reviews and posts each draft in the TikTok app.

How data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • The app does not collect data about other TikTok users, does not sell or share any data, and does not use data for advertising.
  • The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok's settings, after which the stored tokens stop working and can be deleted.

Contact

Questions can be raised at github.com/ikoomm/plottwistplannet-app/issues.
